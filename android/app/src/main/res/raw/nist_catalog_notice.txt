NIST National Vulnerability Database - Official CPE Dictionary
https://nvd.nist.gov/products/cpe

This application uses a filtered snapshot of public hardware product identifiers
and English product titles from the NIST Official CPE Dictionary, retrieved via
the official CPE API on 2026-11-14. The CPE Dictionary is a structured product
naming scheme, not a complete consumer inventory or a discovery fingerprint set.

NIST states that nongovernmental organizations may use the dictionary voluntarily
and that it is not subject to copyright in the United States. NIST attribution
is retained. This application is independent and is not endorsed by NIST.

Software, deprecated, ambiguous and many component-only records are excluded.
A catalogue match does not establish the presence of a device or a vulnerability.
Only current scan responses establish presence. No vulnerability data is bundled.
See android/nist-hardware-provenance.json and android/product-catalog-provenance.json
in the corresponding source distribution for snapshot hashes and selection rules.
