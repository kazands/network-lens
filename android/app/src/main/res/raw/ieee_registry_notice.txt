IEEE Registration Authority public MAC-prefix registry facts.
Sources: https://standards.ieee.org/products-programs/regauth/
Historical distribution statement: https://sources.debian.org/src/ieee-data/20240722/debian/copyright/
This bundled snapshot contains only assignment prefixes and registered organization names. It is not an IEEE endorsement. Registered organizations can be interface suppliers, not the device brand.
Exact sources, retrieval dates and data digests are retained in android/ieee-vendor-provenance.json in the source distribution.
